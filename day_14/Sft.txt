what does in SFT(Supervised Fine-Tuning) trainer, packing = false mean, what does it do, how does it help in our training.

In SFTTrainer, packing=False means each example is used as an independent input instead of combining several into one.
For example, if max_seq_length=100 and your dataset has two samples — one with 30 tokens and another with 50 — each will be padded separately to 100 tokens.
This keeps the instruction–response pairs clean and avoids mixing them together.
It slightly reduces efficiency because of extra padding but ensures the model learns correctly from each example.
So, packing=False helps maintain accuracy and clarity in supervised fine-tuning.
